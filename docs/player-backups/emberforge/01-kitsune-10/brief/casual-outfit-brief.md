Draw Kitsune #10 (Emberforge Foundry playmaker) off the pitch, in casual everyday clothes. This art is for story scenes, relationship scenes and her digital home world.

**Style:** same "Saturday Anime" look as the main menu key art. Crisp two-tone cel shading, bold clean ink lines, warm glow on her core. Feel of a classic 2000s 2D sci-fi film (Atlantis, Treasure Planet, Titan AE era).

**Same character:** same face, flame-red high ponytail with gold hair tie, side bangs, amber eyes, light skin. Goggles worn on her head like a headband.
**Normal look:** NO fox ears and NO red face mark (those appear only while her ability is active). No football kit, shoulder pads, knee guards or match gloves.

**Outfit (see concept sheet for colours):**
1. Knee-length sundress in ember rust #B5442A (shade #7E2B1C): wide straps, fitted bodice, flared skirt. Thin gold #F3AF2C piping on the neckline and hem, a few small cream #F4E6CF fox-flame motifs along the hem. Her chest core glows amber #FF9E24 softly through the bodice.
2. Cropped dark cocoa #442C32 bomber jacket worn open (or slipping off one shoulder), crimson #922123 rib collar, cuffs and hem with a thin gold stripe, gold zip, small gold club patch on the left sleeve.
3. Thin dark cocoa belt with a gold buckle.
4. Small fox-tail charm hanging from the belt (flame red #D42D30 to a cream tip).
5. Cream high-top sneakers, gold panel, red laces, gum sole #B87A4A; crimson ankle socks with two gold stripes.
6. Gold wristband on the left wrist.

**She's a robot, but subtly:** thin gold ring at the base of her neck, core glow through the tee, faint ring highlight in her irises, fine panel seam on forearm, knee and shin. Keep it subtle; she should read as a human girl at first glance.

**Pose and mood:** relaxed, confident, cheeky. One hand on her hip, weight on one leg, warm teasing grin, skirt and ponytail mid-swish. Sporty girl in a dress: feminine but still her.

**Deliver (in this order, stop after each for approval):**
1. One full-body standing illustration, front 3/4 view, at least 2000 px tall, transparent background (real alpha, not a checkerboard), no text, no shadow.
2. After approval: a version for the digital home world. Same pose and outfit, seams faded out, softer and warmer core glow, light rim glow along her edges.

Do not crop her from a presentation board. Keep an editable layered source file.
