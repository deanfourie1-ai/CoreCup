# Kitsune #10: the other five run directions

SE is approved (Nov 15). Do the other five the same way. Same look and same quality bar.

## What worked for SE (repeat it)

- Separate clean drawings on a transparent background, one PNG per pose. **Not** cut from a presentation board.
- Same character, kit, scale and framing as the approved SE drawings. Draw her at the same size on the same canvas (1145 x 1374 is fine). I scale, line her up on her hips and import.
- 6-8 poses of a lively run: contact, compression, passing, push-off, flight, reach.
- No background, shadow, ground line, glow or labels.

Also, if possible: **a 4-pose idle for all six directions** (breathing, ready stance), since SE still uses an older idle.

## Order

Send **E first**. I'll test it, then the rest can follow together.

## How each one is judged

I put it in the game and record it at the normal match camera and normal speed, running at the centre and near the edges. Verdict: **approve / one targeted fix / hold**. Small foot slide or imperfect legs that can't be seen during play are fine. Must fix: visible skipping or hopping, size changes, clipping, missing or cut-out-looking parts, stray pixels.

## After that

Fox Mirage, the Kitsune ability: cast pose, decoys and swap. Close-ups get a higher bar, because players will see those details.
