# Tidewell Mariners: concept sheets needed (design agent brief)

Tidewell has no character art yet. The Kraken, Fenrir and Kelpie sheets in the creature-core zip show **Emberforge** players in maroon and gold, so don't reuse those characters. The Kraken defender also looks too much like Kweku. Draw five new people.

## What to make, per player

One concept sheet in the same style as the Emberforge sheets:
- Portrait
- Five-view turnaround: front, SW 3/4, side, back and NE 3/4
- Six detail close-ups
- One signature ability panel

Use a dark background and add no extra text. Claude cuts each sheet into the shared reference-board template.
